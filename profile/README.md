# Synchain

**音乐制作协作的开源工具。**
**Open-source tools for music production collaboration.**

---

Synchain 是一站式音乐/音频制作协作平台。平台本体是闭源产品,但**跑在你自己机器上的那部分**全部开源 —— 插件、桥接、命令行工具。它们要进你的 DAW、读你的工程、拿你的凭据,你应该能看见它们在做什么。

Synchain is a collaboration platform for music and audio production. The platform itself is a closed-source product, but **everything that runs on your own machine** is open source — the plugins, the bridge, the CLI. If it loads into your DAW, reads your session, or holds your credentials, you should be able to read its source.
